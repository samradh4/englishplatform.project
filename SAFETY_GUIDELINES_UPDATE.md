# Voice Room Safety Guidelines Update

The pre-join voice-room disclaimer was expanded and summarized to cover:

- Personal, contact, social media, financial, identity-document, password and OTP safety
- Private groups, unknown links, payments, promotions and scams
- Respectful English-learning conversations
- Harassment, abuse, threats and inappropriate content
- Recording and screenshot consent
- Impersonation and online identity risks
- Leaving and reporting unsafe users

Users must accept the guidelines before their microphone can start.
