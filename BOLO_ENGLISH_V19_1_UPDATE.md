# Bolo English V19.1

## Trial-room freedom

- New accounts receive an automatic 1-day trial.
- During the active trial, member-created rooms open immediately without administrator approval.
- During the active trial, members can join any approved room across Levels 1, 2, and 3.
- Pending, rejected, closed, locked, full, and banned-room protections remain enforced.
- When the trial expires, room access stops until the administrator activates paid validity.
- Paid members return to level-matched joining and normal room-approval rules.
- Dashboard labels and buttons update automatically according to trial status.
